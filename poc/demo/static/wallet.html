Set up this account

Each action runs the real prototype. Account files live in poc/demo/.sandbox; the demo manages a dedicated THS test network.

The demo will start and manage its own THS environment.

    Owners

    2 of 3 required

    No owner holds a key that can spend on its own, and no complete spending key exists anywhere, not even briefly during setup. Each holds a share; two shares produce one ordinary signature the network cannot tell from a single signer's.

    Can a bad proposal get signed?

    An owner is handed transaction bytes by someone else. Approving the wrong bytes is the whole risk. Pick a field: the account rebuilds the pending transaction with that one thing changed, hands it to Alice, and shows what she does. She must reject it, and no signature may be produced.
