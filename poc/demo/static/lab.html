Research prototype · sandboxed regtest · not a wallet

Run it yourself

This runs the real prototype against a throwaway chain in poc/demo/.sandbox on port 18242. Its own config, its own state, one run at a time, and the chain dies when this server does. Nothing here touches your tracked harness config or any other zebrad.

1 · Build a payment

Each button runs one real stage. They are in dependency order: a later one will tell you what it needs if you skip ahead. The whole sequence takes a few minutes, most of it mining and generating the zero-knowledge proof.

2 · Try to slip something past the signer

This is the interesting one. Pick a field; the lab rebuilds candidate AC with that one field changed, hands it to Alice, and shows what she does. A structurally valid, correctly randomised transaction that is not the payment she agreed to. She must refuse, and no share file may appear. Run the honest control too: without it, a refusal proves nothing, because a signer that refuses everything would also pass.
