03 · Create

Create a vault

Pick a threshold and the number of members. The roster and threshold become immutable once setup begins — changing a member later starts a fresh vault.

◇This vault needs 3 of 5 members to approve any payment. Any 3 eligible members can complete a proposal.

Invites

The creator is member 1 by default. Send each invite privately. Each invite is for one member and one app and expires in 24 hours.

The vault is a draft with no receive address until every member joins, the roster matches, and all back up.

10 · Recover & leave

Recover or leave

Restore your own share on a new device, or remove a member. Changing a label never changes the cryptographic roster.

Restore on a new device

Needs both your 24 words and your final recovery card. The personal-wallet seed alone cannot restore a Loki share.

ℹRestore resets to a fresh nonce epoch and restores the same vault address. It does not remove or disable your old device.

Leave or remove a member

Leaving, or removing a compromised member, creates a new vault and sweeps funds into it. The old roster cannot be edited in place.
